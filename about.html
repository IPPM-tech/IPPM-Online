Welcome to IPPM

The Institute of Public Peace and Management (IPPM) is an international professional membership institute committed to advancing sustainable peace, ethical leadership, professional excellence, innovation, research, capacity development and strategic partnerships.

Our programmes are designed to strengthen individuals, institutions and societies by promoting knowledge, collaboration and responsible leadership that contribute to lasting peace, socioeconomic development and human prosperity.

Legal Status

The Institute of Public Peace and Management (IPPM) is a non-profit professional institute registered in Nigeria as a Company Limited by Guarantee.

Established in 2012, the Institute operates with the approval of the Federal Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Education, promoting professional knowledge, research, management excellence and sustainable peace through education and strategic partnerships.

Our Vision

International Outlook

IPPM is committed to building a globally connected professional community. The Institute promotes multilingual engagement in English, French and Spanish while establishing partnerships with universities, polytechnics, governments, professional bodies, development organizations and international institutions.

Through conferences, professional certification, consultancy, research and international exchange programmes, IPPM seeks to become a recognised global platform for peace, management and sustainable development.
