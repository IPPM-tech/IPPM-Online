Welcome to the IPPM Professional Development Centre

The Institute of Public Peace and Management (IPPM) coordinates executive education, professional development, leadership training, research seminars, conferences and international knowledge exchange programmes designed to strengthen professionals, institutions and communities.

Our Professional Development Series (PDS) equips participants with practical knowledge, strategic insights and internationally relevant skills that contribute to peace, productivity and sustainable national development.
