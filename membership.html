Why Become an IPPM Member?

Membership of the Institute of Public Peace and Management (IPPM) provides access to a distinguished international community of professionals committed to advancing peace, ethical leadership, management excellence, research, innovation and sustainable development.

Whether you are a graduate, public servant, executive, entrepreneur, academic or development practitioner, IPPM provides opportunities for continuous professional growth, global networking and meaningful societal impact.

Benefits of Membership

🏆 Professional Recognition

Receive recognised professional membership designations.

🌍 Global Networking

Connect with professionals and institutions worldwide.

📚 Capacity Development

Access executive education and continuous learning.

🎖 Professional Certification

Participate in structured certification programmes.

💼 Consultancy Opportunities

Benefit from collaborative projects and advisory engagements.

✈ International Exposure

Access conferences, exchange programmes and strategic partnerships.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can become an IPPM Member?

Professionals, graduates, executives, entrepreneurs, academics, public servants, security personnel, development practitioners and qualified individuals from Nigeria and around the world.

Can international applicants join?

Yes. IPPM welcomes qualified applicants from every country.

Is online induction available?

Yes. Selected induction programmes are available virtually.

Can membership be upgraded?

Certainly. Members may progress through higher professional grades after meeting the prescribed requirements.

Are professional designations recognized?

Professional designatory letters are conferred in accordance with the Institute's regulations and membership category.
